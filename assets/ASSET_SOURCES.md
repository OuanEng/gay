# Added level assets

- `psx_interior/`: selected models and textures from the user-provided `PSX modular house interior pack.zip`. Used for beds, bookcases, sofa, tables, and tabletop props.
- `tiny_kitchen/`: selected models from the user-provided `Tiny_Treats_Charming_Kitchen_1.1_FREE.zip`. The included `License.txt` states CC0. Used for the kitchen and table settings.
- `fantasy_trees/`: `TreesPack.glb` and palettes from the user-provided `FantasyStylizedPack.rar`. Used for trees and shrubs around the stages.
- `toon_shader/`: `stylized.gdshader` from the user-provided `SourceCode.zip`. Used on the loft cache.

The PSX, Fantasy, and shader archives did not include a license file in the supplied files. Confirm their redistribution terms before publishing these assets separately.
